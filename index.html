What We Do

Friends Looking Out for Friends

Cayuga's Watchers is a student-led 501(c)(3) nonprofit working to reduce the harms of high-risk drinking at Cornell. We provide honest, research-backed bystander intervention training — created and led by students, for students, and completely free of charge.
