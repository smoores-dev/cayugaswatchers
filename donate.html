Support Our Mission

Every gift keeps our training free for the students and organizations who need it.

Unlike other collegiate bystander intervention programs, Cayuga's Watchers operates independently of Cornell University as an entirely separate 501(c)(3) nonprofit. We rely on the generosity of other nonprofits, charitable organizations, and individual contributors to pay our student trainers — while remaining completely free of charge to student organizations.

Will you consider donating to help us continue to grow and improve? Feel free to reach out to us at hello@cayugaswatchers.org with any questions about our current operations, future plans, or anything else you'd like to know.
